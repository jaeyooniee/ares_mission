    🔴 MARTIAN MAP · EVA PLANNER

    Habitat transparent display → helmet / rover HUD
    Terrain: real HiRISE DEM of the Gale team area (10 km, 16 m grid from a 1 m source). Routes are precomputed by the Python pipeline. Imagery: HiRISE colour mosaic (USGS, processed colour, not a true-colour photo). Grey-strip areas (no colour data) use estimated colour from brightness, not measured.

    Pick a landing site on the start screen (Gale, Jezero, Mawrth Vallis, Meridiani Planum, or Custom with your own DEM, image and CRISM data), or switch later with Location ▼ in the top bar. Then pick objectives in Science value (right): the route visits the best two places for them. Check the GO / NO-GO badge, preview the traverse, then start the first-person helmet or rover HUD. MISSION, ROUTE and CREW open their cards on the left; data overlays (imagery, elevation, slope, cost map, CRISM minerals, science value, and at Gale the Curiosity drill sites and traverse) are in LAYERS.

    Map controls

    • Left-drag — move the map position
    • Right-drag — tilt and rotate the view
    • Mouse wheel — zoom in and out at the cursor
    • Location ▼ (top bar) — switch to Gale, Jezero, Mawrth Vallis, Meridiani Planum or your custom site
    • Click a drill-site pin (Gale, LAYERS → Curiosity drill sites) — its measured minerals

    Touch screen: one finger moves the map, two fingers zoom and rotate.

    Data used in this build

    Elevation · slopeHiRISE DEM of Gale (1 m source, shown at 16 m)
    ImageryUSGS HiRISE colour mosaic of Gale (colour strips fused with the 25 cm red mosaic) — processed colour
    Routes · budgetPlanned in the page: A* on the slope / cost grid of the DEM, with the team pipeline's models (Tobler walking speed × suit factor, Pandolf metabolic O₂ at Mars gravity; rover speed and Wh/km)
    MineralsMRO CRISM summary parameters
    Science valueTeam science.py: HiRISE DEM (geology, reachability), CRISM summary parameters (life, water), THEMIS thermal inertia (resources), Curiosity traverse (novelty), scored against NASA Moon to Mars objectives
    Ground truth (Gale)Curiosity CheMin drill sites: NASA PDS CheMin RDR and PDS Analyst's Notebook (clay wt% from Thorpe et al. 2022); Curiosity traverse: NASA MMGIS. Shown in LAYERS on the map only, as reference: not used in route scoring
    Custom sitesBuilt in your browser from your own files with the same rules as the built-in sites (DEM; optional image, CRISM MTRDR, THEMIS; rover traverse from NASA MMGIS). Nothing is uploaded; the site is kept in this browser and can be downloaded as a package
    Not in this buildSoft soil, boulders, live space weather (DONKI), radiation, rover photos

    Data: NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona (HiRISE) · colour mosaic: USGS Astrogeology (T. Parker, F. Calef III) · colour is processed, not a true-colour photo.
